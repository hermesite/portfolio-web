# Scholarly Platforms
### Interactive platforms for research institutions and university networks

*I collaborated with universities to gain a better understanding of their data. In some cases, this involved internal data, while in others, it involved orienting or focusing on an internationalisation activity. I even partipated in the creation of a tool to help universities plan their future as part of the European Alliances Project.*

I designed and developed accessible frontend interfaces for structured research data. I balanced clarity for general users with depth for analysts.

**The challenge:** Present complex research metadata in ways that serve both expert analysts and general audiences.

**The outcome:** Accessible, responsive interfaces that enable exploration of institutional data and collaborative relationships.

#### Essentials

- Comparative benchmarking and collaboration exploration
- Search and filtering on structured metadata
- Accessible and responsive interface patterns
