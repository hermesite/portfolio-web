### Challenges

- **Complex data, broad audience.** Make large R&I datasets and repositories easy to explore for both technical and non-technical users, balancing visual appeal with functional depth and without oversimplifying the domain.
- **Consistency at scale.** Keep several regional platforms coherent while allowing each of them to adapt and evolve.
- **Speed of delivery.** Automate design and development processes so teams spend their time finding better ways of communicating complex information.
- **Public-sector constraints.** Meet government technical, regulatory and accessibility requirements.
