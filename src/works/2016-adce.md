*I was involved in defining and developing the interactive, browser-based catalogue of creatives and their projects. The result was an integrated digital archive that complemented the traditional printed catalogue.*

**Main focus:** 
- Use visualizations as interactive web components.
- Pixel perfect frontend integration.
